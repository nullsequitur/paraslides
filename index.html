paraslides

Parallel decks for extra slides, parallax depth, and paragliding through 3D slides all at once.

Press Space or Enter to step forward linearly, or use standard Vim navigation: l / h for columns (pages) and j / k for rows (sections).

Spatial Concept

Slides as a Vim Document

In paraslides, your deck behaves exactly like a text document in Vim:

Sections are Rows (Y)

Moving vertically with j / k changes lines while preserving your horizontal column position (sticky column desiredX).

Pages are Columns (X)

Moving horizontally with h / l traverses pages on the line. Moving past the end wraps smoothly into the next row!

Detours are Depth (Z)

Supplementary deep-dives live on the Z-axis without cluttering the primary line.

Presentation Flow

Linear Progression with Space & Enter

Presenters don't always want 2D branching during a talk. Space and Enter provide seamless linear progression:

Within a row, pressing Space advances to the next column. At the end of a section, it automatically wraps to column 1 of the next row. Try pressing Space right now to advance to Section 1!

Seamless Traversal

Radar Wrapping Cues

Notice the directional radar in the HUD pill below:

Left Edge Wrap (↰)

At the start of any section (Col 1), the left arrow displays ↰ indicating pressing h wraps to the end of the previous row.

Right Edge Wrap (↳)

At the final page of a section, the right arrow displays ↳ indicating pressing l wraps to the start of the next row.

Detour Depth 2/3 • Math & Physics

Spatial Mathematical Formulation

Slide coordinates in 3D Euclidean space are governed by discrete affine transforms:

$$\mathbf{P}(y, x, z) = \begin{bmatrix} (x - x_0) \cdot W \\ (y - y_0) \cdot H \\ (z - z_0) \cdot D \end{bmatrix}$$

Power Navigation

Vim Jumps & Numeric Counts

Navigate instantly across your presentation using standard Vim keystrokes:

Row Boundaries

0 jumps directly to column 1.
$ jumps to the last column and enables sticky end-of-row cursor tracking.

Section Jumps

gg jumps to row 1.
G jumps to the final row.
3gg jumps to row 3.

Count Multipliers

Type a number before a command:
3l moves 3 slides right.
2j moves 2 sections down.

Bird's-Eye View

Dedicated Overview Menu (m)

Press m at any time to open the full 2D presentation map overlay.

Strict Grid Navigation

Use h, j, k, l to move your selection cursor without jumping across lines.

Confirm Selection

Press Enter or Space to jump directly to the highlighted slide and close the menu.

Abort & Restore

Press m or Esc to close without altering your current slide position.

Status Indicators

Vim Statusline & Fixed HUD Pill

The bottom-right status bar maintains constant dimensions to eliminate visual jitter:

- Coordinates: Col x/X • Row y/Y in Main track, and Col x/X • Dep z/Z in Detour track.
- Morphing Key: Displays grayed-out [i] when no detour is available, active [i] when detours exist, and active [o] inside detours.
- Directional Radar: 4-way arrow compass with wrap indicators (↳ and ↰).

Reference Guide

Complete Navigation Matrix

h / l Move Left / Right across columns (pages). Wraps sections at boundaries.
j / k Move Down / Up across rows (sections). Preserves sticky column position.
Space / Enter Linear forward step. Wraps sections automatically. (In menu: confirms selection).
i Enter Detour sequence (active when slide has attached depth slides).
o / Esc Exit Detour sequence back to parent slide. (In menu: aborts without moving).
0 / $ Jump to start / end of current row.
gg / G Jump to top row / bottom row of presentation.
[count]key Multiplier prefix (e.g. 3l, 2j, 5gg).
m Toggle 2D Overview Map overlay.
Ctrl + P Print presentation to PDF (interleaved or main-only layouts).
