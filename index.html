Press Space or Enter to step forward linearly, or use standard Vim navigation: l / h for columns (pages) and j / k for rows (sections).

Spatial Concept

Slides as a Vim Document

In paraslides, your deck behaves exactly like a text document in Vim:

Pages are Columns (X)

Moving horizontally with h / l traverses pages on the line. Moving past the end wraps smoothly into the next row!

Sections are Rows (Y)

Moving vertically with j / k changes lines while preserving your horizontal column position (sticky column desiredX).

Detours are Depth (Z)

Supplementary deep-dives live on the Z-axis without cluttering the primary line.

Power Navigation

Vim Jumps & Numeric Counts

Navigate instantly across your presentation using standard Vim keystrokes:

Row Boundaries

0 jumps directly to column 1.
$ jumps to the last column and enables sticky end-of-row cursor tracking.

Section Jumps

gg jumps to row 1.
G jumps to the final row.
3gg jumps to row 3.

Count Multipliers

Type a number before a command:
3l moves 3 slides right.
2j moves 2 sections down.

State Integrity

Sticky Column & Cursor Memory

Every vertical transition preserves or restores horizontal column context intuitively:

Sticky desiredX

If you navigate to column 5 and move down into a short 2-page section, paraslides clamps to page 2. Moving into a wider section immediately restores column 5!

lastX[y] Memory

Each row remembers your last visited column. Returning to a previously visited section immediately restores your exact previous location.
